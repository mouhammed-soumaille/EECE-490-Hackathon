Data: 33,887 Stories orders · models trained on Jan–Apr, tested on June · discounted-vs-full-price is an observed link, not a controlled test

Before building anything we tested the three ideas most teams would try. One: copy what people usually buy together. There are no real product pairs, so that gives random suggestions. Two: a 10% combo. Discounted orders didn't add more food, so the discount is just lost margin, about $3,900 a year. Three: a complex AI model to predict who adds food. It scored no better than a simple rule, so we kept the simple rule.
