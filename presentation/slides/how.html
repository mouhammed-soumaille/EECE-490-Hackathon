How it works

One suggestion per order, chosen for profit

1 · Ring

Cashier rings the first item: a cappuccino at Harbour, 8:40 am.

2 · Score

Every food item × every discount, priced at this branch, net of people who’d buy anyway.

3 · Suggest

“Make it a Morning Pairing?” One line, one price, or nothing at all.

4 · Learn

Accept or decline is logged. Each night the engine updates what it believes.

Day one: a 135-row table

Branch × first item → suggestion and price. Loads into any POS. No servers.

Guardrails Stories controls

Max 15% off, 40% minimum margin, add-on ≤ 2.5× the first item, no repeat offers.

No customer data

Works from the basket alone. Nothing personal is stored or needed.

Four steps. The engine scores every possible add-on and discount for this exact branch and suggests at most one. Day one it is a lookup table any till can read. From then on it learns from every accept and decline.
