The pilot

Eight weeks to replace every assumption with a measurement

WEEKS 1–2

Log every answer

Staff make the playbook suggestion and tap accept or decline. ~2,200 suggestions pin acceptance to ±1 point.

WEEKS 3–8

Odd/even receipt A/B test

Even receipt numbers get a suggestion, odd ones don’t. No customer data, nothing for staff to remember. Six weeks detect ~$0.18 extra gross profit per order; the base case predicts $0.25.

Stop rule

Acceptance under ~2% after week 2: stop. The engine can’t earn its keep.

Go rule

Gross profit per order higher in the suggestion arm at 5% significance: roll out.

Then switch on learning

v2 runs on the logged answers; discounts tested on one branch only.

Power analysis: 80% power, 5% significance, order-level gross-profit SD $2.79, ~187 orders a day (notebook 05)

The pilot is cheap and decisive. Two weeks of logging tell us acceptance directly. Six weeks of odd-even receipts give a clean causal answer on profit. The stop and go rules are written down before we start.
