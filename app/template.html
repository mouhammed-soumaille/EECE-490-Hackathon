Measured

Who pairs, and with what

Pairing varies with what is rung first, not with branch or hour. A matcha pulls bakery 78% of the time; a sandwich pulls a cold drink 41% of the time. No product-level pair survives testing, so the engine picks the category from data and the item by profit.

Decided

Profit, not popularity

Each option is scored on expected extra gross profit at that branch, net of customers who would buy anyway. Garden's 1.43× unit costs change its best suggestion. Discounts are used only when they pay.

Learned

Pilot, then learn every night

Two weeks logging accept/decline, then six weeks of odd/even receipts as an A/B test. After that, v2 updates its beliefs nightly from the till log: no retraining, no customer data.
